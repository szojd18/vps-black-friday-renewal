## Are these Black Friday VPS discounts real? Three tests

Run these three before you believe any percentage on a Black Friday landing page.

A tracker that re-checked on November 13 found $6.49 is the same 24-month introductory price Hostinger has charged since March 2026, and that the struck-through figure is a reference price rather than what the plan used to cost. The number that matters to your budget is $155.76 today and $11.99/mo at renewal.

**2. The renewal multiple.** This is where most of the money sits. Hostinger renews at 1.7 to 2.2 times the sale price. ScalaHosting renews at 2.4 to 3.6 times. AccuWebHosting's VPS Power goes from $9.73/mo to $39.12/mo, a clean 4.0×. ScalaHosting has a second wrinkle: the sale covers managed Cloud plans only, needs a one-year term, and existing customers renewing don't qualify, so the discount is a new-customer price.

**3. The cheapest regular plan with the same RAM.** Hostinger's KVM 1 gives you 4 GB for $6.49/mo. Time4VPS sells 2 vCPU and 4 GB for $3.79/mo on a one-year term, $45.48 upfront, with no sale attached. The tracker's summary of the nine sale plans it priced: none costs less per month than the cheapest regular plan with as much RAM at a provider that isn't running a sale. ScalaHosting is the harder case to judge, because its official page doesn't show what the same plans cost on the same term outside the sale, so the size of the cut can't be measured. What can be measured is the $54.95/mo renewal against the $22.46/mo you start on.

## Four things to confirm before you pay

1. **The term and the exact amount charged today.** Hostinger's price only exists on 24 months, Bluehost's and HostGator's extra 15 to 25% only on 12-month terms, and ScalaHosting's only on one year. Prices are also quoted excluding VAT, which gets added at checkout for many buyers.
2. **The renewal price, in writing.** Anything renewing above about 2× the introductory rate needs a plan for year two. If you'd move before then, price the migration in.
3. **Managed or unmanaged.** Hostinger, HostGator, HostArmada, Namecheap and RackNerd are self-managed. ScalaHosting, Liquid Web and InMotion lean managed. A $22/year VPS plus 20 hours of your time is not cheaper than a $22/month managed plan if you bill your time at anything.
4. **The refund window.** Hostinger advertises 30 days. HostArmada's VPS refund window is only 7 days, which is short for judging network quality. ## FAQ

Black Friday falls on Friday, November 27, 2026, and Cyber Monday on Monday, November 30, 2026. Plenty of VPS sales open weeks earlier and close before the day itself.

**Does "$6.49/mo" mean I pay $6.49?**
No. It's $155.76 charged once for 24 months. The monthly figure is the total divided by the term.

**How much will my renewal go up?**
Hostinger renews at 1.7 to 2.2× the sale price, ScalaHosting at 2.4 to 3.6×, AccuWebHosting at 4.0×. RackNerd's 1 GB plan renews at the price you sign up on.

**Why can't I see a VPS price on Bluehost or HostGator?**

**Is 1 GB of RAM enough for a website?**
For a static site or a small container, yes. For WordPress with a normal plugin stack, 2 GB is the sensible floor.
